# Authoritative Source Whitelist

Comprehensive guide to authoritative sources by domain.

## Tier 1A: Academic Monographs (Highest Authority)

### European Traditions

**German Historical School**
- Borgolte, Michael. *Das europäische Mittelalter im Spannungsbogen des Vergleichs*. Berlin: Akademie Verlag, 2014.
- Mitteis, Heinrich. *Der Staat des hohen Mittelalters*. Weimar: Böhlau, 1940.
- Brunner, Otto. *Land und Herrschaft*. Vienna: Rohrer, 1939.

**English Legal History**
- Maitland, Frederic William. *The History of English Law Before the Time of Edward I*. Cambridge, 1895.
- Milsom, Stroud Francis Charles. *The Legal Framework of English Feudalism*. Cambridge, 1976.
- Baker, John Hamilton. *An Introduction to English Legal History*. London: Butterworths, 2002.

**French Historical School**
- Bloch, Marc. *La société féodale*. Paris: Albin Michel, 1939-1940.
- Duby, Georges. *Les trois ordres ou l'imaginaire du féodalisme*. Paris: Gallimard, 1978.
- Le Goff, Jacques. *Pour un autre Moyen Âge*. Paris: Gallimard, 1977.

### Sinology and East Asian Studies

**Japanese Sinology**
- 夫馬進 (Fuma Susumu). *中国善会善堂史研究* (Studies on the History of Chinese Benevolent Institutions). Kyoto: 同朋舎, 1997.
- 宫崎市定 (Miyazaki Ichisada). *中国史* (Chinese History). Kyoto: 筑摩書房, 1978.
- 滋賀秀三 (Shiga Shūzō). *中国家族法の原理* (Principles of Chinese Family Law). Tokyo: 創文社, 1967.

**English Sinology**
- Ebrey, Patricia Buckley. *The Cambridge Illustrated History of China*. Cambridge, 2010.
- Hansen, Valerie. *The Open Empire: A History of China to 1800*. New York: W.W. Norton, 2015.
- Lewis, Mark Edward. *The Early Chinese Empires: Qin and Han*. Cambridge: Harvard University Press, 2007.

### Other Traditions

**Italian Legal History**
- Calasso, Francesco. *Medioevo del diritto*. Milan: Giuffrè, 1954.

**Dutch Economic History**
- de Vries, Jan, and Ad van der Woude. *The First Modern Economy: Success, Failure, and Perseverance of the Dutch Economy, 1500-1815*. Cambridge, 1997.

## Tier 2: Conditionally Permitted (With Caveats)

### Chinese Academic Journals (CNKI Core)
**Permitted with International Comparison**:
- Must verify through CNKI (cnki.net)
- Must be CSSCI-indexed (Chinese Social Sciences Citation Index) or CSCD (Chinese Science Citation Database)
- Must cross-reference with Web of Science/Scopus equivalent
- Never use if only abstract available without methodology section

### Government White Papers
**Permitted if**:
- Official government publication channel
- Identified as white paper (白皮书)
- Not classified or confidential

### Think Tank Reports
**Selectively Permitted**:
- Brookings Institution
- Peterson Institute for International Economics
- RAND Corporation
- 国务院发展研究中心 (DRC)
- 中国社会科学院 (CASS)

**Must verify**:
- Funding sources and potential conflicts
- Methodology transparency
- Peer review status

## Tier 3: Use with Extreme Caution

### News Organizations (Never as Primary Source)
**May cite for**:
- Direct quotes from officials (attribute to official)
- Breaking news confirmations (must verify with official source within 24h)
- Event timing (must cross-reference)

**Never cite for**:
- Statistics without primary source
- Analysis without attribution
- Anonymous source claims

### Academic Working Papers
**Use if**:
- From established institution (NBER, CEPR, etc.)
- Author has strong publication record
- Clearly labeled as "preliminary" or "draft"

**Must include**:
- Working paper status disclaimer
- Institution and date
- Link to latest version

## Quick Reference: Source Selection Decision Tree

```
Is the information:
├── Statistical data?
│   ├── China? → stats.gov.cn + OECD/World Bank cross-check
│   ├── US? → BEA/BLS/Census + cross-check
│   └── Other? → National stats office + IMF/WB
├── Legal/Regulatory?
│   ├── China? → flk.npc.gov.cn
│   ├── US? → Cornell LII or official source
│   └── Other? → Official national database
├── Academic claim?
│   ├── Natural science? → Peer-reviewed journal (WoS/Scopus)
│   ├── Social science? → Top-tier journal or academic monograph
│   └── Humanities? → Academic monograph + international reviews
├── Historical?
│   ├── Chinese history? → Int'l Sinology + Chinese authoritative
│   └── Other? → Academic monographs + peer-reviewed articles
├── Technical/Engineering?
│   ├── Patent information? → USPTO/EPO/JPO/CNIPA
│   └── Standards? → ISO/IEC/IEEE official
└── Corporate?
    ├── Public company? → SEC EDGAR / CNINFO
    └── Private company? → Official announcements only
```
